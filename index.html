PROJECT 02
SPATIAL DATA SCIENCE
UCL · Individual dissertation · April–August 2023

Spatial Data Analysis
of London High Streets

Using retail and socioeconomic datasets to identify patterns and drivers of commercial change

Project overview

Investigated how London's high streets changed over time by combining ten years of socioeconomic data with more than 10,000 retail records. The analysis explored patterns of commercial change and the socioeconomic and accessibility-related factors associated with different high-street trajectories.

Work Summary

Led the end-to-end analytical workflow, including data preparation, integration, feature development and machine-learning classification. Used Python and GeoPandas to structure and analyse the datasets, and scikit-learn to develop classification models. Optimised the data-processing workflow, improving efficiency by approximately 20%.

Data & methods

10 years of socioeconomic data · 10,000+ retail records · spatial data integration · classification modelling

Tools

Python · pandas · GeoPandas · scikit-learn

PROJECT 03
MARKET & URBAN CHANGE
UCL · Team project · September 2022–January 2023

Impact of COVID-19 on
Airbnb Supply & Demand in London

Building a reproducible Python analysis to investigate changes in the short-term rental market

Project overview

Worked in a team to conduct exploratory data analysis of the London Airbnb market at borough level, comparing 2019 (pre-pandemic) with 2022 (post-pandemic). The report examined supply using listing counts and availability, and used review recency as an indicator of demand.

The report found that listing counts rose in 29 of 33 boroughs, while the number of listings classified as in-demand fell in 29 boroughs. The spatial analysis also indicated a shift in supply and demand activity towards outer London. These are descriptive findings from the project’s exploratory analysis, not results from a formal predictive model.

My contribution

Helped define the briefing structure and analytical framework, proposed revisiting the research question and reviewing relevant literature when the initial implications were unclear, and compared analytical results to identify evidence supporting the team's key findings.

Co-developed the coding for the analysis in Python and Jupyter Notebook, covering data loading, preprocessing and analysis. I structured and annotated the workflow, recorded technical challenges and iteratively tested solutions in discussion with the team to produce reproducible analytical steps.

Focus

London Airbnb market · COVID-19 · supply and demand changes

Tools & methods

Python · Jupyter Notebook · data ingestion · preprocessing · reproducible analysis · literature review
